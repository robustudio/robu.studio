04 · Tracking

Every shift,
every number.

Takings, cash and card, daily pay (yevmiye), expenses, km and cash left — by day, week or month, by vehicle and by driver.

Figures come from the shifts the driver saves in Taksi Hesap. A shift shows up once the driver saves it.
