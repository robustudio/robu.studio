Gece Vardiyası (Night Shift) — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Gece Vardiyası mobile game (package name com.dorukstudio.gecevardiyasi), published by Robu Studio.

In short: the game never sends any data to us or anyone else. The profile you create and your night progress are stored only on your own device.

Data that reaches us

None. The game has no online accounts, ads, analytics or third-party tracking. It never asks for or collects your email, real name, location, contacts, photos or device identifiers.

Permissions and internet

The game uses only the vibration permission, to vibrate the phone during jump scares. This permission is granted automatically, gives access to no information, and vibration can be turned off in the game's Settings. The game has no internet permission: it runs entirely offline and cannot send data off your device.

Profile stored on your device

When you start, you create a profile: a username (a nickname is fine), a password and your age. This is kept only on your device, in the app's own storage; it is never sent over the internet and never reaches us. Several profiles can be created on the same device.

Your password is not stored in readable form; only a one-way hash is kept. The password is used only to open that profile on that device. Because the game is offline, a forgotten password cannot be recovered.

Game progress stored on your device

Each profile's progress (the next night, the highest night completed, the chosen difficulty, unlocked achievements, notes read and custom night settings) is also kept only on your device and never reaches us. Sound, look sensitivity and vibration settings are stored on the device as well.

If Android backup is turned on, your profile and progress may be backed up to your Google account through Google's backup service. That backup is between you and Google; we have no access to it. You can turn backup off in your device settings.

Data sharing

Since no data ever leaves your device, no data is shared with, sold or transferred to third parties.

Purchases

There are no real-money purchases and no payment details are ever entered in the game.

Children's privacy

The game never sends any user's information off the device, whatever their age. The age asked for in the profile also stays on the device and is not used for ads, profiling or anything else.

Deleting a profile and progress

You can delete a profile and its progress with Şifremi unuttum (Forgot password) on the sign-in screen. To delete all profiles and progress at once, go to Settings → Apps → Gece Vardiyası → Storage → Clear data on your device, or uninstall the app.

Changes to this policy

If a feature that sends data off the device is ever added, this page will be updated and its "Last updated" date changed.
