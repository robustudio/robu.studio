Gece Vardiyası (Night Shift) — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Gece Vardiyası mobile game (package name com.dorukstudio.gecevardiyasi), published by Robu Studio.

In short: the game does not ask for, collect, store or send any personal data.

Data we collect

None. The game has no accounts, sign-up, ads, analytics or third-party tracking. It never asks for or collects your name, email, age, location, contacts, photos or device identifiers.

Permissions and internet

The game requests no permissions on your device. It does not have internet permission either: it runs entirely offline and cannot send data off your device.

Data stored on your device

The game does not write any save data to your device. Night progress resets when the app is closed.

Data sharing

Since no data is collected, no data is shared with, sold or transferred to third parties.

Purchases

There are no real-money purchases and no payment details are ever entered in the game.

Children's privacy

The game does not collect information from any user, whatever their age.

Changes to this policy

If a feature that collects data or sends it off the device is ever added, this page will be updated and its "Last updated" date changed.

Contact

Questions: support@robu.studio
