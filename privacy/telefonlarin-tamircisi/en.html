Telefonların Tamircisi — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the mobile game Telefonların Tamircisi ("The Phone Repairer", package name com.kayastudio.telefontamir), published by Robu Studio.

In short: the game does not send any data to us or to anyone else. The profile you create and your game progress are stored only on your own device.

Data that reaches us

None. The game has no online accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking software. It never asks for or collects your email, real name, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or any similar information.

Permissions and internet access

The game requests no permissions from your device. It has no internet permission either; it runs fully offline and cannot send data off your device.

Profile stored on your device

When you start the game you create a profile: a username (it can be a nickname), a password and your age. This information is stored only on your own device, inside the app's private storage; it is never sent over the internet and never reaches us. More than one profile can be created on the same device.

Your password is not stored in readable form; only a one-way hash of it is kept. It is used solely to open that profile on that device. Because the game is offline, a forgotten password cannot be recovered.

Game progress stored on your device

Each profile's progress (in-game money, XP, spare parts in stock and the equipment bought) is also stored only on your device and never reaches us.

If Android's backup feature is turned on for your device, the profile and save data may be included in the backup that Google's backup service stores in your own Google account. That backup is between you and Google; we have no access to it. You can turn backup off in your device settings.

Data sharing

Because no data leaves your device, no data is shared with, sold to or transferred to any third party.

In-game money

The "₺" amounts shown in the game are play money only. The game has no real-money purchases and never asks for payment details.

Children's privacy

The game does not send any user's information off the device, regardless of age. The age asked for in the profile also stays on the device; it is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Deleting your profile and save

You can delete a profile and its progress with the Şifremi unuttum ("Forgot my password") option on the sign-in screen. To delete all profiles and saves at once, on your device open Settings → Apps → Telefonların Tamircisi → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app.

Changes to this policy

If a feature that sends data off your device is ever added to the game, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date will change.
