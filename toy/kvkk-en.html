KVKK Disclosure Notice

Last updated: November 16, 2026

In accordance with Article 10 of Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (the “Law”) and the Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles to be Followed in Fulfilling the Obligation to Inform, we provide this notice about how your personal data is processed when you use the Toy app.

In short: the data controller is Belemir Leşkeri (an individual, trading as Robu Studio). We process your email address, display name, posts and technical logs to provide your account and the community features, keep the service secure and meet legal obligations. We do not process data for advertising and we do not sell it. Data is kept on servers in Frankfurt; this is a transfer abroad within the meaning of Article 9 of the Law. You can exercise your rights under Article 11 of the Law by writing to us.

1. Data controller

Your personal data is processed within the Toy app by Belemir Leşkeri (an individual, trading as Robu Studio) as the data controller defined in the Law. Contact: support@robu.studio

2. Personal data processed

  • Identity: display name.
  • Contact: email address.
  • User transactions: account type, registration date; posts, comments, likes, saved posts, community memberships, event and live attendance/reminder choices, live chat and private message contents, blocking, report and notification records.
  • Visual and audio recordings: photos and videos you attach to a post.
  • Creator application: name, category, description, links, applicant type, verification code and review note.
  • Transaction security: IP address, sign-in and request times, app and device type, moderation decisions about your account.
  • Special categories of personal data (health, religion, ethnic origin, sex life, etc.) are not something we aim to collect and we do not ask for them. But if you share such information in your posts, comments and photos of your own accord, remember that it may be public; we recommend that you do not share it.

3. Purposes of processing

  • To create your membership account, verify your identity and let you sign in,
  • to provide the community, post, comment, messaging, event and live announcement features,
  • to review creator applications and carry out invite and verification procedures,
  • to keep the service secure; to prevent spam, abuse and unlawful content; to review reports and take moderation decisions,
  • to detect and fix errors and keep the service running,
  • to meet legal obligations and requests from competent authorities, and to establish, exercise or protect a right.
  • Your personal data is not processed or sold for marketing, advertising or profiling.

4. Legal grounds

Your data is processed on the following grounds in Article 5(2) of the Law: the establishment and performance of a contract (providing your account and the community service, subparagraph c); compliance with a legal obligation (subparagraph ç); the data having been made public by you (your public posts, subparagraph d); the establishment, exercise or protection of a right (subparagraph e); and legitimate interests (service security, preventing abuse and moderation, subparagraph f — provided your fundamental rights and freedoms are not harmed).

5. How the data is collected

Data is collected through the Toy app and its infrastructure, by automatic or partly automatic means, from the information you enter in the app and from your use of it. If you sign in with Google, your email address and account ID are obtained from Google.

6. Who the data is transferred to

Service providers (abroad): Toy obtains its database, authentication and file storage from Supabase infrastructure; the data is kept in the Frankfurt (Germany) data centre on Amazon Web Services. Supabase is a company headquartered in the USA. If you choose “Continue with Google”, data is shared with Google for authentication (and with Apple if you choose “Continue with Apple” in the iPhone version).

Your personal data is therefore transferred abroad within the meaning of Article 9 of the Law. The transfer is made on one of the grounds listed in Article 9 and only to the extent needed to provide the service. Using Toy requires these service providers; if you do not want your data to be transferred abroad, you should not create an account.

Domestic transfers: none. Public authorities: if a request that complies with the law is made by a competent authority, to the extent the law requires.

Other users: your display name, posts, comments and likes are visible to other users of the app and to visitors who are not signed in; what you write in a live chat is visible to the members of that community. This is not a transfer but a disclosure that you choose to make. See the Privacy Policy for details.

7. Retention period

Your data is kept while your account exists and as long as needed for the purpose of processing. When you delete your account, your account and the data tied to it are deleted immediately; deleted data may remain for a short time in our infrastructure provider's routine backups and logs. Records that are subject to a legal retention obligation or a dispute are kept for the relevant period and are then deleted, destroyed or anonymised.

8. Your rights under Article 11 of the Law

As a data subject you have the right to:

  • learn whether your personal data is processed,
  • request information if it has been processed,
  • learn the purpose of the processing and whether it is used in line with that purpose,
  • know the third parties to whom it is transferred in Türkiye or abroad,
  • ask for it to be corrected if it is incomplete or inaccurate,
  • ask for it to be deleted or destroyed under the conditions in Article 7 of the Law, and ask that this be notified to the third parties to whom it has been transferred,
  • object to a result against you that arises from analysis of the processed data exclusively by automated systems,
  • claim compensation if you suffer damage because of unlawful processing.

9. How to apply

To exercise these rights, write to support@robu.studio from the email address registered to your account, saying which right you want to exercise and giving enough information for us to identify you. Your application is concluded free of charge as soon as possible according to the nature of the request and within 30 days at the latest; if the process requires an extra cost, the tariff set by the Personal Data Protection Board may apply. In the app you can edit your display name, delete your posts and close your account. If your application is rejected, the answer is insufficient or no answer is given in time, you keep the right to complain to the Personal Data Protection Authority.

10. Contact and updates
