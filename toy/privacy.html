Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This policy applies to the Toy mobile app (package name tr.toy.community) developed by Belemir Leşkeri (an individual, trading as Robu Studio). The notice that Turkish data protection law (Law No. 6698, “KVKK”) requires is set out separately in the KVKK Disclosure Notice.

In short: Toy is an app where fans meet in the communities of artists and creators. If you create an account, your email address, your display name and the content you share are stored on a server. Your posts, comments, likes and display name are public; your private messages are visible only to the person you are talking to. There are no ads, no tracking and no analytics tools; we do not ask for camera, microphone, location or contacts permissions; we do not sell your data. The servers are in Europe (Frankfurt). You can delete your account from the app at any time.

1. What data we process

  • Account: your email address and password. The password is stored only in a one-way encrypted form; we cannot see it. If you sign in with Google, the email address and account ID that Google reports are processed; Google does not give us your password.
  • Profile: the display name you choose, your account type (fan, creator, administrator) and your registration date.
  • What you share: your posts (text, and optionally a photo or video), comments, likes, saved posts, the communities you joined, your “going” marks for events, live reminders and what you write in a live chat.
  • Private messages: your conversations with a creator.
  • Security and moderation records: the accounts you block, the reports you file (the reason and which content), the notifications you receive and the decisions the moderation team takes about your account (for example removing a piece of content or suspending the account).
  • Creator application: if you apply, the name, category, description, links, applicant type and the verification code the system gives you. If you use an invite code, the code itself is not stored; only a digest of it and the fact that it was used are stored.
  • Technical logs: our infrastructure provider's server logs (IP address, time of the request, app and device type, sign-in times). They are kept for security and troubleshooting.
  • What stays on your device: the token that keeps you signed in and your app preferences are kept in your phone's own storage.

2. Who can see it

Public (including people who are not signed in; it can also be read through the app's public data interface): your display name and account type, your posts and comments, which posts you liked, community pages, and event, live and product announcements. Photos and videos are stored at public links; anyone who knows a link can open it. For that reason, do not share your face, your address, your phone number or images and details of other people. Hidden data inside the files you upload (for example location, EXIF) may not be removed automatically; we recommend that you do not upload photos tagged with a location.

Visible only to certain people: what you write in a live chat is visible to the members of that community. Your email address, the communities you are a member of, your saved posts, your blocked accounts, your notifications and your event and reminder choices are not shown to other users. Your private messages are visible only to the two people in the conversation; the moderation panel does not show messages. The person with technical access to the database (the developer of the app) may look at the messages concerned only when a legal obligation, a security incident or the review of a report requires it.

Visible to the moderation team: to manage accounts, the moderation panel shows, in the user list, your name, email address, account type, registration date and suspension status; your creator application; reports (including the name of the person who filed the report and the reported content) and the log of moderation actions.

3. Why we use the data

  • To create your account and let you sign in,
  • to provide the community features: posts, comments, likes, messaging, event and live announcements,
  • to review creator applications and give the “Official” badge (“Resmî” in the app) to verified communities,
  • to prevent spam, abuse and unlawful content, review reports, and remove content or suspend accounts when needed,
  • to meet our legal obligations,
  • to find and fix errors.

4. Who processes the data

Supabase (database, authentication and file storage service): data is kept on servers in the Frankfurt (Germany) region, in Europe. Google: only if you choose “Continue with Google”, for signing in. Apple: only in the iPhone version, and only if you choose “Continue with Apple”, for signing in. These providers process data under their own privacy policies and their agreements with us.

We do not use an ad network, analytics or tracking tools. We do not sell your data or share it with anyone for marketing; we do not build profiles or take automated decisions. If an authorised public authority makes a request that complies with the law, we provide information to the extent the law requires.

Because data is processed on servers outside Türkiye, the details about international transfers are in the KVKK Disclosure Notice.

5. Permissions and device access

Toy connects to the internet for your account, the communities and uploading files. It receives a photo or video through your phone's own picker, only for the file you choose. It does not ask for camera, microphone, location, contacts or phone notification permissions. We do not send push notifications to your phone at the moment; your notifications appear inside the app.

6. Retention and deletion

Your data is kept as long as your account exists. You can delete a post or a comment at any time. In the app, Profile → Delete my account permanently and immediately deletes your account and everything tied to it (posts and the files you uploaded, comments, likes, saved posts, messages, memberships, your notifications and your reports); see the account deletion page for details.

Deleted data may remain for a short time in our infrastructure provider's routine backups and logs. An anonymous record of moderation decisions, and records that must be kept because of a legal obligation or a dispute, may be kept for as long as required.

7. Your rights

Under Article 11 of Turkish Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and, if you live in the EU, the GDPR, you have the right to learn whether your data is processed, to ask for information, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to its processing and, where possible, to take it with you. You can edit your display name, delete your posts and close your account in the app; for other requests write to support@robu.studio from the email address registered to your account. You also have the right to complain to the competent authority (in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Authority).

8. Children

Toy is not aimed at children under 13; children under 13 may not create an account. If you are between 13 and 17, you must open the account with the knowledge and permission of your parent or guardian. If we notice an account that belongs to someone under 13, we close it; if you see such an account, write to support@robu.studio.

9. Security

Communication with the server is encrypted (HTTPS). The database rules are set up so that each person can change only their own records and can read their private data only themselves (or, for a conversation, the parties to it); people you block and you cannot see each other's content. The number of uploads, the number of reports and the message rate are limited. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, this page is updated and the “Last updated” date changes. If we want to use your data for a new purpose, we will announce it in the app first.
