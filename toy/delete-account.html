Delete Your Account

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Here is how to delete your Toy account and the data tied to it.

In short: in the app, Profile → Delete my account; or email us. Deletion is immediate and permanent, and cannot be undone.

Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Toy and tap the Profile tab at the bottom.
  2. Tap Delete my account in the list.
  3. If your account has a password, enter your password and type SİL (Turkish for “delete”) to confirm; if you signed up with Google, type SİL and verify again with Google (a fresh sign-in is needed within a short time).
  4. Your account and the data tied to it are deleted immediately and permanently. This cannot be undone.

If you cannot get into the app

Send an email titled “Toy account deletion” from your account's email address to support@robu.studio. After verifying that it is you, we delete your account within 30 days at the latest.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in record: your account ID, your email address and your sign-in provider; your profile (display name).
  • Your posts and the photo and video files you uploaded; your comments; your likes and saved posts.
  • Your community memberships, event attendance, live reminders and your messages in live chats.
  • Your private messages: they are also deleted from the other person's screen.
  • The accounts you blocked, the reports you filed and your notifications; your creator application and (if you are a creator) the event, live and product entries you announced.
  • Comments and likes that other people made on your posts are deleted along with your posts.

What may remain

  • If you are a creator account: the community page you created is not deleted; it is left without an owner and the “Official” badge is removed. If you want the page removed completely, write to support@robu.studio.
  • An anonymous moderation record of the deletion itself, and records that must be kept because of a legal obligation or a dispute.
  • Copies that remain for a short time in our infrastructure provider's routine backups and logs.
  • Posts and comments that other people wrote about you are their content; deleting your account does not delete them.
