5. Important note about patterns and measurements

Sewing patterns are drafted with standard formulas and the measurements you enter; they are basic blocks made for an average body. Every body and every fabric is different, and small differences are normal. We recommend sewing a test version in inexpensive fabric before cutting your real fabric.

When printing the PDF choose “actual size / 100%”, and check the 5 × 5 cm square on the first page with a ruler. The scale of patterns traced from a photo depends on the quality of the photo and on how accurately you mark the A4 sheet; compare the resulting measurements with the garment.

We give no assurance that patterns, measurements, printouts or cut fabric will be error-free, fit for a particular purpose or fit your body exactly.
