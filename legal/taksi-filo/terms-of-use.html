1. Description of the service

Taksi Hesap Filo lets taxi owners and tenants ("managers") follow their vehicles and drivers in one place. A manager adds vehicles by plate and sends an invite link to their drivers. If a driver accepts, a summary of the shift records the driver already keeps in Taksi Hesap (date, km, takings, daily pay, expenses) appears read-only on the manager's screen.

Taksi Hesap Filo is a separate app from Taksi Hesap; you do not need Taksi Hesap Filo to use Taksi Hesap. Taksi Hesap's own Terms of Service apply to it separately.

The app is not an accounting, tax, legal or employment-advice service. The records on screen are what the driver entered in Taksi Hesap; we do not verify their accuracy. Plates are user declarations: we do not ask for proof of ownership and do not verify plates.

3. Your responsibilities as a manager (owner / tenant)

  • You add only vehicles you have the right to manage and real plates; you do not present someone else's plate as your own.
  • You send an invite link only to the driver you are inviting. Accepting sharing is entirely up to the driver; you do not pressure them to accept.
  • You use the shift records you see only to manage vehicles and drivers; you do not share or disclose them to third parties and you comply with data-protection law (including the KVKK).
  • You accept that when a driver stops sharing or you remove a driver, your access ends immediately and you will no longer see those records.

4. Your consent and withdrawing it, as a driver

  • An invitation comes with an acceptance screen that states the manager's name, the plate and what will be visible; sharing starts only if you accept. An invite link is valid for 7 days.
  • The manager sees only the records of that plate and only the shift summaries from the days after you accept (date, km, takings, daily pay, expenses); earlier records and your other plates are not shared, and they cannot see your Taksi Hesap profile or other data.
  • You can stop sharing at any time, plate by plate, on the Sharing screen; access ends immediately. The manager can also remove you; for a removed driver to share again the manager must send a new invitation.
  • Taksi Hesap Filo does not change your Taksi Hesap records and does not copy them to its own server; it only shows them on the manager's screen.
  • As a driver you pay nothing.
