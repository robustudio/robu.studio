🚕 Taksi Filo — Privacy Policy and KVKK Disclosure Notice

Last updated: October 26, 2026
🌐 Türkçe sürüm
Under Turkish Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data ("KVKK"), this notice explains how the Taksi Filo mobile app (Android and iOS), developed by Belemir Leşkeri (an individual) as data controller, handles your personal data. Contact: support@robu.studio. By using the app you confirm that you have read this notice.

1. What Taksi Filo is

Taksi Filo lets taxi owners and tenants (managers) follow their vehicles and drivers in one place. Drivers can choose to share the shift records they already keep in Taksi Hesap with a manager. Taksi Filo is a separate app from Taksi Hesap; you do not need Taksi Filo to use Taksi Hesap.

2. Data we collect

  • Sign-in data: when you sign in with Google or Apple, your account e-mail, the name the provider gives us and a unique user ID (UID). The sign-in system is shared with Taksi Hesap: the same account works in both apps.
  • Profile: full name, optional phone number, your role (taxi owner / tenant / driver) and language.
  • Vehicles (if you are a manager): the plates you declare, optional nicknames and an owner/tenant label per vehicle. We do not ask for or verify ownership documents; a plate is your own declaration.
  • Driver invites (if you are a manager): the invited person's name and optional e-mail/phone, the invite's plate and expiry. An invite also carries your name and, if set, your phone so that it can be shown on the consent screen.
  • Sharing (if you are a driver): which plate's records you share with which manager and from which date; when you accepted and which version of the text you accepted.
  • Your Taksi Hesap shift records (if you are a driver and accepted sharing): the manager sees only your shift records for the shared plate from the day sharing started: date, km, takings (cash/card), daily pay (yevmiye), expense items and trip count. These records stay in Taksi Hesap; Taksi Filo does not copy them to its own servers, it only displays them on the manager's screen.
  • Check on the consent screen: before you accept an invite we look at whether that plate is in your own Taksi Hesap profile. This check is only for your own account, its result is shown only to you, and nothing is stored.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data. We use no advertising ID, show no ads, do not track you across apps or sites and do not sell your data.

3. Who sees your records, and when

  • A manager can see a driver's records only if the driver accepted the invite. The consent screen states who is asking, which plate and what will be visible.
  • The manager sees only that plate's records and shifts from the day you accepted; earlier records and your other plates are not shared. They cannot see your Taksi Hesap profile or other data.
  • A driver can stop sharing at any time, plate by plate, from the Sharing screen; a manager can also remove a driver. In both cases access ends immediately. A removed driver can share again only after the manager sends a new invite.

4. Why we use the data and the legal basis

  • To create your account and manage your session,
  • To provide vehicle and driver management,
  • To share a driver's records with a manager with the driver's consent,
  • To answer support requests, keep the app running and secure.

Legal bases: for account, profile and vehicle/driver management data, necessity for the performance of a contract (KVKK art. 5/2-c); for sharing a driver's shift records with a manager, explicit consent (KVKK art. 5/1) — given on the consent screen and withdrawable at any time; for security, our legitimate interest (KVKK art. 5/2-f). Data is collected electronically through the mobile app, directly from you, and through Google/Apple sign-in services.

5. Service providers and transfers abroad

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore): sign-in and storage of the data above. A Firebase project shared with Taksi Hesap is used; Taksi Filo data is kept in its own collections.
  • Google Sign-In and Sign in with Apple: authentication.

These providers' servers may be outside Türkiye, so your data may be transferred abroad. They are subject to their own privacy policies. Your data is not shared with other third parties (except records shared with a manager with the driver's consent) and is not disclosed unless legally required.

6. Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account is open. You can delete your account any time in the app under Settings → Delete my account (you will be asked to sign in once more for security). This immediately deletes your Taksi Filo profile, vehicles, invites and sharing. If you also have a Taksi Hesap account, that account and its records are not touched and your sign-in record is kept for Taksi Hesap. If you do not use Taksi Hesap, your sign-in record is deleted too. Details: Account deletion.

Records a manager wrote in their own account (for example the invite they wrote for you) are that manager's data and are deleted when they delete their account.

7. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) provided by Firebase and is protected by access rules: a user can read only their own data, and a manager only the records a driver has approved. No method is 100% secure, but we take reasonable technical measures.

8. Children

The app is aimed at professional taxi operators and drivers and is not directed at children under 13; we do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Your rights (KVKK Article 11)

Under Article 11 of the KVKK you may contact us to:

  • Learn whether your personal data is processed,
  • Request information about it if it is,
  • Learn the purpose and whether it is used accordingly,
  • Know the third parties your data is transferred to, in Türkiye or abroad,
  • Ask for correction if it is incomplete or inaccurate,
  • Ask for deletion or destruction when the conditions are met,
  • Ask that corrections/deletions be notified to third parties the data was transferred to,
  • Object to a result against you arising solely from automated analysis,
  • Claim compensation if you suffer damage from unlawful processing.

To exercise these rights, contact support@robu.studio; requests are resolved within 30 days at the latest, as required by the KVKK. Your GDPR rights apply in the same way.
