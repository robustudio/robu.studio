🚕 Taksi Filo — Account Deletion

Last updated: October 26, 2026
🌐 Türkçe sürüm
You can delete your Taksi Filo account and data at any time. This page explains how and what gets deleted.

Delete from the app (recommended)

  1. Open Taksi Filo and go to the Settings tab.
  2. Tap Delete my account and confirm.
  3. For security you will be asked to sign in once more with Google or Apple. Deletion completes after you do.

What is deleted

  • Your Taksi Filo profile (name, phone, role),
  • The vehicles you added (plates, nicknames),
  • All invites you created (including the invited person's name, e-mail and phone),
  • The links for records you share as a driver (managers can no longer see them) and the driver links you manage as a manager.

If you also have a Taksi Hesap account

Taksi Filo and Taksi Hesap use the same sign-in system. If you have a Taksi Hesap account, that account and your shift/cash records are not affected; only your Taksi Filo data is deleted and your sign-in record is kept for Taksi Hesap. To delete Taksi Hesap too, use the Settings of the Taksi Hesap app. If you do not use Taksi Hesap, your sign-in record is deleted as well (and, if you signed in with Apple, the Apple authorization is revoked).

What remains in someone else's account

Records a manager wrote in their own account (for example the invite note they wrote for you) are that manager's data and are deleted when they delete their account.

If you cannot access the app

Write to support@robu.studio from the e-mail address linked to your account; your request is resolved within 30 days at the latest. See the Privacy Policy for details.
