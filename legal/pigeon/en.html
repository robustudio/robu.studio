🕊️ Pigeon — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026
🌐 Türkçe sürüm · Privacy Policy · Terms of Use · Delete your account
This notice explains how the Pigeon app (Android), developed by Belemir Leşkeri (an individual) as data controller, handles your personal data, including under Turkish Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (KVKK) and the GDPR. Contact: support@robu.studio. By using Pigeon you confirm that you have read this notice.

1. What Pigeon is

Pigeon is a slow pen-pal app. You write letters to people around the world, and each letter takes time to arrive: the farther apart you are, the longer it takes. A carrier bird of your choice delivers it. You can also collect stamps and keep a private daily journal.

2. Data we collect

  • Sign-in data: you sign in with Google. We receive your account e-mail, the name and picture Google provides, and a unique user ID.
  • Your profile: nickname, Pigeon ID, gender, birth date (you choose whether other people see the full date or only your zodiac sign), country and city that you enter yourself, bio, avatar (an emoji or a photo you upload; uploaded avatar photos are visible to other users), interest topics, languages, answers to profile questions, writing habits, matching preferences (such as age range, preferred gender, excluded topics, blocked countries) and your privacy and notification settings.
  • Letters: the text, any photo and any voice note you send; when you sent it, when it arrives, the distance between the two countries, the carrier bird, and the read status. A letter can be read only by its sender and recipient, and the recipient can read it only after it has arrived. Moderators and administrators cannot open private letters.
  • Pen pals and safety: who you write with, which pen pals you star or hide, who you block, and reports you file (the text you write and who you report).
  • Open letters, "message in a bottle" requests and letter circles: the content you post or send through these features and your matching choices.
  • Community stories (optional): if you submit a story, it is reviewed by us and, if approved, shown publicly, under your display name or anonymously as you choose. We also store when you gave consent.
  • Daily Pages (your private journal): mood, note, caption and photo. Only you can see them.
  • Virtual items: your coin balance and transactions, stamp collection, achievements and the stamps you showcase on your profile (the showcase is visible to other users). Pigeon does not take real-money payments in this version.
  • Saved texts and drafts: reusable paragraphs and unsent drafts. If you export your data, the export file is kept for you privately.
  • Technical data: our backend provider receives your IP address and basic device and connection details in server logs, as any online service does.

Permissions: the microphone is used only when you record a voice note; photos are used only when you pick a picture through the system picker. Pigeon does not read your GPS location (your country and city are what you type in), your contacts or your camera. We use no advertising ID, show no ads, run no analytics or tracking tools, do not track you across apps or sites and do not sell your data.

3. Why we use the data and the legal basis

  • To create your account and run the app, deliver your letters with the distance-based delay, and show your profile to other users according to your settings,
  • To suggest pen pals and circles based on the interests, languages and preferences you chose,
  • To keep Pigeon safe: handling blocks and reports, warning or suspending accounts that break the rules,
  • To answer support requests and to keep the service running and secure.

Legal bases: necessity for the performance of a contract (KVKK art. 5/2-c; GDPR art. 6(1)(b)) for account, profile and letter features; our legitimate interest in security and abuse prevention (KVKK art. 5/2-f; GDPR art. 6(1)(f)); and your explicit consent for optional items such as community stories and uploaded photos, which you can withdraw at any time. Data is collected electronically, directly from you through the app and through Google Sign-In.

4. Who can see your data

  • Other users: your public profile (nickname, avatar, country, bio, interests, languages and the items you chose to show) is visible to other Pigeon users. Things such as your birth date, last seen time and join date follow the privacy settings you choose. Your letters are visible only to the recipient.
  • Moderators / administrators: can see profiles, reports and the moderation log to handle abuse. They cannot read private letters.
  • Service providers: Supabase (database, sign-in system and file storage) and Google (Google Sign-In). They process data on our behalf under their own policies.
  • We do not sell your data and do not share it with advertisers. We disclose data to authorities only if the law requires it.

These providers' servers may be outside Türkiye or your own country, so your data may be transferred abroad.

5. Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account is open. You can delete your account at any time in the app under Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, letters (including the letters you sent, so recipients lose them too), photos, voice notes, pen pals, journal pages, stamps and coins. Details: Delete your account. A short-lived copy may remain in provider backups until it is overwritten.

You can also export your data from the app (Settings → Account → Export your data).

6. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Access rules in the database limit each user to their own data and to what is meant to be shared with them; letters are not released to the recipient before they arrive. No method is 100% secure, but we take reasonable technical measures.

7. Children

Pigeon is for people aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from younger children; if you believe a child has created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Under KVKK Article 11 and the GDPR you may ask us to:

  • Tell you whether your personal data is processed and give you a copy,
  • Explain the purpose, and who it is shared with, in Türkiye or abroad,
  • Correct incomplete or inaccurate data (you can also edit your profile yourself),
  • Delete or destroy your data (or use the in-app deletion),
  • Restrict or object to processing, and receive your data in a portable form,
  • Withdraw consent you gave, and claim compensation for damage from unlawful processing, as the law provides.

To exercise these rights write to support@robu.studio; we answer within 30 days at the latest. You can also complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK) or your local data protection authority.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy; significant changes will be announced in the app or on this page, and the date above shows the last update. Contact: Belemir Leşkeri — support@robu.studio

🕊️ Pigeon — Terms of Use

Last updated: November 2, 2026

1. Using Pigeon

You must be at least 16 years old, keep your account information accurate, and keep your Google account secure. One person, one account.

2. Your content

You own what you write and upload. You give Pigeon permission to store it and deliver it to the people you send it to, as the app works. You are responsible for what you send. Do not send anything illegal, or anything you do not have the right to share.

3. Community guidelines

  • Be kind. Pen pals come from every background: write the way you would want a stranger to write to you.
  • No harassment, hate speech, threats or unwanted contact. Block and report anyone who makes you uncomfortable.
  • No spam, scams, advertising or solicitation, and no impersonation.
  • No sexual content involving minors or exploitation of any kind; it will be reported to the authorities.
  • Respect the wait: letters take time to arrive by design. Do not try to get around it.

4. Moderation

We may warn, limit or suspend accounts that break these terms or the guidelines, and remove content that is reported and found to be harmful. We do not read private letters; we act on reports, which include what the reporter chooses to tell us.

5. Virtual items

Coins and stamps are virtual items with no cash value. They cannot be exchanged for money and may be changed, rebalanced or removed as the app develops.

6. The service

Pigeon is provided "as is", without warranties, and may change or be interrupted. To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for indirect losses. Nothing here limits rights you have under mandatory law.

7. Ending your use

You can delete your account at any time (see Delete your account). We may end access for serious or repeated breaches.

8. Changes and contact

We may update these terms; continued use after a change means you accept it. Contact: support@robu.studio
