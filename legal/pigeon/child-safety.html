🕊️ Pigeon — Child Safety Standards

Last updated: November 2, 2026
🌐 Türkçe sürüm · Privacy Policy and Terms
Pigeon has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). This page describes the standards we hold Pigeon to and how we respond when something is reported. Pigeon is published by Belemir Leşkeri (an individual), contact: support@robu.studio.

1. Who can use Pigeon

Pigeon is for people aged 16 and over. It is not designed for children, and we remove accounts we learn belong to someone under that age.

2. What is not allowed

  • Any child sexual abuse material, in any form: images, video, audio or text.
  • Any sexualization of a minor, or any sexual or romantic approach towards someone under 18.
  • Grooming: building trust with a minor in order to exploit them, including asking a minor for images, meetings or secrecy.
  • Sexual extortion, trafficking, or any other exploitation of a child.
  • Sharing or linking to any of the above.

Accounts that break these rules are banned. See also our Community Guidelines.

3. How people can report a concern

  • In the app: open a conversation (or a pen pal in your Friends list), tap the ⋯ menu and choose Report, then pick a reason and add details. You can also Block anyone from the same menu at any time, which ends the conversation.
  • By e-mail: write to support@robu.studio. We treat anything involving a child as urgent.

4. How we respond

  • Our moderators review every report. They can warn an account or ban it permanently, and a ban stops it from sending letters.
  • Where we become aware of child sexual abuse material or the sexual exploitation of a child, we report it to the competent authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) where applicable and the law enforcement bodies of the relevant country, and we cooperate with their investigations.
  • When we report to the authorities, we give them the information we hold about the account and the report.

5. Private letters

Letters in Pigeon are private: only the sender and the recipient can read them, and moderators cannot open them. For that reason we do not scan letters; we act on reports, on what the reporter tells us, and on information about the reported account. If you receive anything that involves a child, please report it so we can act.

6. Our commitment

We comply with the child-safety laws that apply to us and we review these standards as Pigeon grows. A designated contact is available for child-safety questions from users, authorities and platform operators: support@robu.studio.
