🕊️ Pigeon — Delete your account

Last updated: November 2, 2026
🌐 Türkçe sürüm · Privacy Policy
You can delete your Pigeon account and its data at any time. This page explains how and what is deleted.

Delete from the app (recommended)

  1. Open Pigeon and go to Profile → Settings → Account.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm with Yes, delete everything. There is no undo.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, avatar photo, interests, languages and settings,
  • Your letters, including the letters you sent (the people you wrote to lose them too), with their photos and voice notes,
  • Your pen pals, blocks, reports you filed and your drafts and saved paragraphs,
  • Your Daily Pages (journal) and their photos,
  • Your stamps, achievements and coins,
  • Your sign-in record in our authentication system.

What may remain

A short-lived copy may remain in our provider's backups until it is overwritten. Conversations are deleted for both sides: when you delete your account, the letters the other person wrote to you in those conversations disappear as well. Community stories you submitted are removed with your account.

If you cannot access the app

Write to support@robu.studio from the e-mail address linked to your Google account. We resolve your request within 30 days at the latest. See the Privacy Policy for details.
