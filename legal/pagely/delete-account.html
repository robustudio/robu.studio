Delete your Pagely account and data

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This page explains how to delete your Pagely account (app identifier studio.robu.pagely) and everything stored with it.

Delete your account from inside the app

  1. Open Pagely and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap the ⋯ button at the top left (a menu opens).
  3. In the menu, tap Account.
  4. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

The account and its data are removed immediately. This cannot be undone.

If you signed in with Apple, Apple asks you to confirm once more before the deletion. That also removes Pagely's access to your Apple ID.

If you cannot open the app

Email support@robu.studio from the address your account uses, with the subject "Delete my account". We verify that the request comes from the account's own email address and then delete it. We reply within 30 days, normally much sooner; under KVKK Art. 13 that is the maximum.

If you signed in with Apple

You can also remove Pagely's access to your Apple ID yourself: on your iPhone or iPad open Settings → your name → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple → Pagely and tap Stop Using Apple ID. That does not delete your Pagely account; use the steps above for that.

What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your profile: display name, username, bio, profile picture and cover photo, country/city, social links and appearance settings,
  • Your library, shelves and reading status,
  • Reading sessions, page progress, streaks and goals,
  • Ratings, reviews, scorecards, notes and any photos attached to them,
  • Posts and their photos, comments and likes,
  • Club memberships, the messages and photos you sent in clubs, your direct messages and your stories (with their photos),
  • The cover photos of books you added by hand, and your export file,
  • Follows in both directions (including authors and publishers you follow), your blocks and your book requests,
  • Reports made about you and warnings given to you, and reports you sent,
  • Your subscription record (plan, status, expiry date, the store's purchase identifier).

What is kept, and for how long

  • Backups: your data disappears from the live system straight away; copies inside the weekly database backups cycle out within 4 weeks at the latest.
  • Moderation action log: the record of which moderation action an administrator took against an account, and when, is kept without any link to your account and contains nothing that identifies you. Reports made about you and warnings given to you are deleted with your account.
  • Payment records: subscriptions are billed by Google Play or Apple, not by us. They keep their own payment and invoice records under tax law, and we have no access to them and cannot delete them. Manage or cancel a subscription at Google Play → Subscriptions (Android) or Settings → your name → Subscriptions (iPhone and iPad).

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it separately in Google Play or in your Apple ID subscriptions, or billing continues.

Deleting only part of your data

You do not have to delete your account to remove things. Each of these deletes on its own, from inside the app, at any time:

  • A shelf: Bookshelf → open the shelf → Delete shelf.
  • A book on a shelf: open the shelf → remove the book from it.
  • A book (from your library): open the book → choose Not added in the status menu and confirm (its progress, notes, quotes and rating are deleted too).
  • A review: open the book → delete your review.
  • A note and its photo: open the book → Notes → delete the note.
  • A post: find the post in the feed or on the book → delete it.
  • A story: open your story → delete it.
  • A comment: open the post → delete your comment.
  • A like: tap the like again to remove it.
  • A club membership: open the club → Leave.
  • A follow: open the profile → Unfollow.
  • A block: Settings → Blocked users → Unblock.

A message you sent in a club cannot yet be removed on its own from inside the app. To have it deleted, email support@robu.studio from your account's address and say what to remove; we action it within 30 days. Deleting your account removes it along with everything else.
