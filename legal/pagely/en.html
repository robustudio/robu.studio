Pagely — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Pagely mobile app (package name studio.robu.pagely) collects, uses and protects your personal data. By using the app you accept this policy.

Data we collect

  • Account: You sign in with your Google account, with Apple, or with an email address and password. From Google we receive your email address and the name and picture on your Google profile; from Apple we receive your name (only the first time you approve) and either your email address or the private relay address Apple creates for you. If you sign in with email, or set a password under Account → Password, we store your email address and your password; the password is not kept in plain text but only as a one-way hash in our authentication service, Supabase, and we cannot see it. The app does not currently send you confirmation or password-reset emails.
  • Profile: Display name, username, bio, profile picture and cover photo; only if you choose to add them, your country, your city and the links you put on your profile (TV-List, Instagram, Goodreads, X and Spotify usernames, a website); and your profile appearance settings (name colour, accent colour, background image).
  • App content: Your library and shelves, reading status and page progress, reading sessions and durations, reading goals, ratings and reviews, scorecards, notes and any page photos attached to them, posts and post photos, comments and likes, club memberships and club messages, direct messages, stories and who viewed them, book requests you send, bookcases you arrange, who you follow and who follows you (including the authors and publishers you follow), and the extra details you record on a book, such as its format, series, price, how you got it and your recommendation.
  • Books you add by hand: Title, author, page count and the cover photo you take.
  • Moderation data: Users you block and reports you send; and, if a report is made about your account or a moderation action (warning, content removal, suspension) is taken against it, the record of that.
  • Export file: If you export your library, reviews and notes (a Premium feature), the resulting file is kept in private storage tied to your account; each export overwrites the same file.
  • Subscription: If you subscribe, your plan, its status and expiry date, and the store's purchase identifier. We never receive your card or payment details.

We never read your device's location; the country and city on your profile are text you choose to enter yourself. Photos you upload are re-encoded before being sent to the server, which strips their EXIF metadata — including GPS coordinates.

Stories: A story stops being shown in the app 24 hours after it is posted. The stored record and photo of an expired story are not shown to others; they stay in your account until you delete the story or your account.

How we use the data

  • To create your account and sign you in,
  • To provide core features such as reading tracking, shelves, notes, goals and statistics,
  • To run social features such as follows, posts, comments and clubs,
  • To prevent abuse (blocking/reporting) and to improve the app.

We do not sell your data to third parties for advertising.

Third-party services

  • Supabase: Used for authentication, the database and file (image) storage. Your data is stored on Supabase's servers in Frankfurt (Germany).
  • Google Sign-In: Used for signing in. Google's own privacy policy applies.
  • Sign in with Apple: Used for signing in on iPhone and iPad. Apple's own privacy policy applies.
  • Google Books API and Open Library: Provide book information and cover images. The titles, authors and search words you look up are sent to these services; your identity is not shared with them.
  • TMDB: Used to find the film or series made from a book, and its cast. Only the book's title is sent. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
  • Wikipedia and Wikidata: Used for author information such as a short biography, birth details and a photo. Only the author's name is sent.
  • Google Play and the App Store: If you subscribe, the store takes the payment. To confirm that the subscription is genuine and whether it is active, our server sends the purchase identifier to the store's own interface (Google Play Developer API, App Store Server API). We keep your plan, its status and expiry date and the store's purchase identifier.
  • Expo: Used for app updates.

As with any internet request, these services can see your device's IP address. The book, film and author lookups above never include your account details.

The app may show a card for TV-List, another app by Robu Studio. Whether TV-List is installed on your device is checked on the device itself and is not sent anywhere.

Camera and photos

We ask for access to your photo library when you set a profile picture, a cover image, a post image, a note photo, or the cover of a book you add by hand. We ask for camera access to scan book barcodes and to photograph pages. Only images you pick or take are uploaded; the camera is never used in the background.

Note, post, story and club-chat photos are kept in private storage and served to users allowed to see that content through short-lived links. Profile pictures and cover photos, and the cover photos of books you add by hand, sit in public storage: anyone who knows the link can view them.

Private account

You can hide your profile with Profile → the ⋯ menu at the top left → Edit profile → Private account. On a private account your shelves, reading activity and follower list are visible only to followers you have approved, and your account does not appear in search. This is enforced server-side with row-level security rules, not merely hidden in the interface.

A narrower option on the same screen is Hide activity: your account stays public and searchable, but your shelves and reading activity are not shown to anyone but you (enforced server-side), and your follower counts are hidden in the app interface.

Keeping and deleting your data

How to delete your account and data →

Your data is kept for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from inside the app: Profile tab → the ⋯ menu at the top left → Account → Delete my account. This permanently and irreversibly deletes your account and all of your data, including your profile, library, shelves, reading history, sessions, goals, notes, posts, comments, likes, messages, stories, club memberships and follows, as well as every photo you uploaded (profile, note, post, story, club chat, covers of books you added by hand) and your export file.

Retention: Deleting your account removes your data from the live system immediately. Weekly database backups are kept on the data controller's own computer and the latest 4 copies are retained; deleted data drops out of these backups within 4 weeks at the latest. Backups are used only to restore data after a loss. Reports made about you and warnings given to you are deleted with your account; the administrator action log (which moderation action was taken, and when) stays, without any link to your account. If you bought a subscription, payment records are held by Google Play or Apple and are subject to statutory retention periods under tax law; we have no access to them. The record of your plan and its expiry date that we keep is deleted with your account.

If you cannot access the app, email support@robu.studio to request deletion.

Getting a copy of your data

You can request a machine-readable copy of the data we hold about you. Email support@robu.studio from your account's address and we will respond within 30 days. In addition, Premium members can export their library, reading sessions, reviews, notes and shelves themselves as a JSON or CSV file from Profile → the ⋯ menu → Account → Export your data; that file does not include social content such as posts, messages and stories, so for a full copy the email route applies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or erased, to receive a copy in a portable format, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Turkish users additionally hold the rights set out in Article 11 of Law No. 6698 (KVKK). To exercise any of these, email us from your account's address; account deletion is also available at any time from Profile → the ⋯ menu → Account → Delete my account.

Data controller: Belemir Leşkeri — Kocaeli, Türkiye — Contact: support@robu.studio

Legal bases: We process your data to perform our contract with you (providing the app you signed up for), to meet legal obligations (moderation and abuse records), and on the basis of legitimate interests (preventing abuse, improving the service).

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and protected by row-level security (RLS) policies; each user can reach only their own data and content made visible to them.

Retention: Your data is kept for as long as your account is active; when you delete your account it is removed from the live system immediately (details in "Keeping and deleting your data" above and on the account deletion page). Records that a legal obligation requires (for example payment records held by the stores) remain with those providers for the period set by the relevant legislation.

Your rights (KVKK Art. 11): You have the right to learn whether your personal data is processed; to request information about it if it has been processed; to learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly; to know the third parties to whom it is transferred in Türkiye or abroad; to request correction if it is incomplete or inaccurate; to request its erasure or destruction; to request that corrections, erasures and destructions be notified to the third parties to whom the data was transferred; to object to an outcome against you produced solely by automated analysis; and to claim compensation if you suffer damage through unlawful processing.

How to apply: To exercise these rights, write to support@robu.studio from the email address registered to your account (your identity is verified through that address). Applications are concluded free of charge and at the latest within 30 days. Deleting your account from Profile → the ⋯ menu → Account → Delete my account in the app is always available too.

Children's privacy

Pagely is not an app aimed at children and does not verify age. If we notice that a child's data is held in an account, or a parent tells us, we delete the account and its data.

Subscription and payments

Pagely's core features are free. A Pagely Premium subscription covers detailed statistics, the monthly and yearly recap posts and the year in review, exporting your library, unlimited shelves (3 on the free plan), unlimited note photos (20 on the free plan), the ability to create clubs, profile personalisation (name colour, accent colour, background) and showing your country and city on your profile. Joining a club is always free.

  • Plans: Monthly and yearly. Current prices are shown in the app and in the store (Google Play or the App Store) at the moment of purchase.

  • Billing and renewal: Payment is taken through your Google Play account (Android) or your Apple ID (iPhone and iPad). The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • Cancelling: Cancel any time from Google Play → Subscriptions on Android, or Settings → your name → Subscriptions on iPhone and iPad. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period you have paid for, and premium features stay available until then. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription.
  • Refunds and withdrawal: Refunds follow the refund policy of Google Play or Apple and are handled by them; we never see your payment details. Any statutory right of withdrawal remains — for digital services that right can be limited once performance has begun, which is why the 7-day free trial is offered.

  • Account deletion: Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. Cancel it separately in Google Play or in your Apple ID subscriptions to stop being charged.
