TV-List — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the TV-List mobile app (package studio.robu.tvlist) collects, uses and protects your personal data. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Data we collect

  • Account: The email address and name Google or Apple gives us when you sign in, plus your profile photo when signing in with Google. We never see or store your password — Google or Apple handles authentication. If you choose "Hide My Email" with Apple, we receive Apple's relay address rather than your real one.
  • Profile: Display name, username, bio, profile and cover photos, any social media handles you add, and optionally a country and city you enter yourself. Location is optional and comes from your own choice, not from your device's location sensor; the app never asks for location permission.
  • App content: Your watchlists, watched episodes/movies, favorites, custom lists, ratings and reviews, notes, comments, posts and any photos you upload, stories that disappear after 24 hours, likes, club memberships and messages, follows/followers, and messages you send to other users.
  • Moderation: Users you block and reports you submit.
  • Subscription status: If you buy Premium, the plan (monthly/yearly), status and period end date as reported to us by the store. We never see or store your card or payment details — payment is taken by Google Play or the App Store.
  • Notification token: If you allow notifications, your device's notification token is stored against your account so we can send them.

How we use data

  • To create your account and sign you in;
  • To provide core features such as tracking, lists, the calendar and statistics;
  • To power social features such as following, comments, posts, clubs and messaging;
  • To prevent abuse (blocking/reporting) and improve the app.

We do not sell your data to third parties for advertising.

Third-party services

  • Supabase: Used for authentication, database and file (image) storage. Your data is stored on Supabase servers.
  • TMDB (The Movie Database): Provides show and movie data. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. No personal data is shared with TMDB.
  • YouTube: Trailers are played via embedded YouTube; YouTube's own privacy policy applies.
  • Expo (EAS): Used for over-the-air app updates and for hosting the web version that shared profile links open.
  • Google Play Billing / Apple App Store: Sell and renew the Premium subscription. The store takes the payment; we only ask the store whether a subscription is valid and store the answer.
  • Google Books: When showing the book a title was adapted from, only the title of the production is queried. No personal data is shared.
  • Apple: Handles authentication if you use "Sign in with Apple".

Photos

We ask for access to your device's photo library when you add a profile picture, a cover image, a post, a story or a photo in a chat. Only the images you pick are uploaded; the app does not access your camera. Post photos are kept in private storage and served to authorised viewers through short-lived links.

Sharing your profile

You can share your profile with a link. It opens /u/your-username on the app's web version and is visible to visitors who are not signed in. A private account stays private; what appears is governed by the per-section visibility settings under Profile → ⋯ → Privacy.

Subscription and payments

Some features are unlocked by a Premium subscription, sold through Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iOS) and renewing automatically.

  • The store takes the payment. We never see or store your card number, billing address or payment details.
  • After a purchase we ask the store to confirm it is genuine. Only the plan, status and period end date are stored from the answer.
  • Managing and cancelling the subscription is done from your store account, under that store's rules.

Notifications

With your permission, two kinds of notification are sent:

  • Generated on the device: New-episode reminders for shows you track are scheduled locally; no data is sent to our servers for these.
  • Sent from our server: New followers, messages, likes, comments and posts from the app's own account. For these, your device's notification token is stored against your account and handled through Google's (Android) and Apple's (iOS) notification services. You can turn individual kinds off inside the app, and withdraw the permission from your device settings at any time.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account is active. You can delete your account any time from within the app: Profile → ⋯ → Account → Delete my account. This permanently and irreversibly deletes your account and all associated data, including profile, lists, watch history, ratings, notes, comments, posts, photos, club memberships, messages and follows. See the account deletion page for a step-by-step guide and what to do if you can't open the app.

Retention: Deleting your account removes your data from the live system immediately. Copies held in backups cycle out within 30 days at the latest.

If you cannot access the app, email support@robu.studio to request deletion.

Getting a copy of your data

You can download a machine-readable copy from inside the app: Profile → ⋯ → Export your data. JSON and CSV are offered, covering your library, ratings, watched episodes and lists. This is part of the Premium subscription.

If you do not have a subscription, or cannot open the app, email support@robu.studio from your account's address and we will respond within 30 days.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or erased, to receive a copy in a portable format, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Turkish users additionally hold the rights set out in Article 11 of Law No. 6698 (KVKK). To exercise any of these, email us from your account's address; account deletion is also available at any time from Profile → ⋯ → Account → Delete my account.

Data controller: Belemir Leşkeri, Kocaeli/Türkiye — Contact: support@robu.studio

Legal bases: We process your data to perform our contract with you (providing the app you signed up for), to meet legal obligations (moderation and abuse records), and on the basis of legitimate interests (preventing abuse, improving the service).

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and protected by row-level security policies, so each user can access only their own data and content made visible to them.

Children's privacy

TV-List is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them. If we learn we've collected data from a user under 13, we delete their account and data.

To exercise these rights, write to support@robu.studio from your account's email address. Applications are answered within 30 days at the latest. For deletion, Profile → ⋯ → Account → Delete my account inside the app is always available.
