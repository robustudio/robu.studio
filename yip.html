01 · Knitting

Draw it, then watch it knit.

Brush, fill, select, repeat — with 96 yarn shades and ready-made colour sets. A stitch-true fabric preview tiles your motif across a swatch, and a float check flags long floats.

  • Photo to chart: matched to the nearest yarn shades
  • Share the numbered chart and yarn table as a PDF

02 · Crochet

Type a round. See it in 3D.

Write your pattern round by round and the piece takes shape. Stitch-count mistakes are pointed out line by line, with abbreviations in your own language.

  • Stitch symbol charts
  • Drag to turn it, pinch to zoom
  • Written guides with materials and steps

03 · Sewing

Patterns drafted to your size.

Skirt, dress, tee, jacket and trousers — in women’s, men’s and children’s sizes. Start from a size chart or enter your own measurements, and adjust length, ease, sleeves, collar and pockets.

  • Live preview with seam allowance, notches, grain lines and darts
  • Women, men and children (86–164 cm)
Women · Men · Child
Print life-size

16 sheets. Tape. Cut. Sew.

The pattern is split into A4 sheets with a scale-check square, a sheet map and alignment marks. Print at 100%, tape the sheets together, cut along the line.

  • Copy a garment from a photo taken next to an A4 sheet
  • Standard formulas: sew a test version in cheap fabric first
